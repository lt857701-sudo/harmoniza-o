## Brand & Style

The design system embodies modern clinical luxury, medical precision, and warm architectural minimalism. Tailored for discerning clients seeking bespoke cosmetic dermatology, laser therapies, and advanced skin rejuvenation, the aesthetic balances high-touch hospitality with clinical mastery.

The visual style blends warm organic minimalism with editorial poise:
- **Atmospheric Calm:** Generous negative space, airy compositions, and unhurried visual pacing establish a sanctuary-like experience.
- **Editorial Contrast:** Crisp, high-end serif display typography set against serene neutral tones creates a sense of legacy and understated authority.
- **Tactile Softness:** Pill-shaped interactive triggers, softened container radii, subtle champagne borders, and rich natural photography evoke skin textures, light, and natural radiance.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from tactile dermatology, warm travertine, raw linen, and golden hour radiance.

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Primary Base: `#FAF8F5` (Soft warm off-white, used across main viewports).
  - Surface Raised / Cards: `#FFFFFF` (Pristine clean white for elevation and content cards).
  - Surface Subdued / Alt Sections: `#F5F2EB` (Warm cream for alternating editorial panels, secondary modules, and stat displays).
- **Text & Foreground:**
  - Deep Charcoal: `#1A1A1A` for commanding headlines, prominent titles, and maximum legibility.
  - Soft Charcoal: `#2D2D2D` for body copy and detailed clinic descriptions.
  - Muted Neutral: `#706E6B` for metadata, captions, and secondary disclosures.
- **Accents & Highlights:**
  - Muted Champagne Gold: `#C5A880` applied selectively to key badges, active tabs, subtle micro-accents, and treatment highlights.
  - Warm Nude / Beige: `#D4C3B3` and `#E8DFD5` utilized for ghost borders, pill dividers, and delicate framing.
  - Obsidian Inversion: `#141414` applied to focused dark-mode statement panels and premium conversion cards.

## Typography

Typography establishes an interplay between high-fashion editorial tradition and clinical legibility:

- **Headlines & Display:** Playfair Display delivers an architectural, timeless atmosphere. Headings maintain a lighter weight (`400`–`500`) with intentional line spacing to preserve breathing room. Uppercase section titles use expanded tracking (`0.12em`) at compact sizes to announce chapters with subtlety.
- **Body & Controls:** Plus Jakarta Sans ensures optical clarity at all sizes. Its refined proportions and open counters deliver clinical legibility across dense ingredient listings, clinical results, and booking flows.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous outer gutters and structural margins.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 768px` (4 columns, `1.25rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters).
  - Tablet: `768px – 1024px` (8 columns, `2rem` outer margins, `1.25rem` gutters).
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12 columns, max-width `1380px`, `3rem` outer margins, `1.5rem` gutters).
- **Rhythm & Structure:**
  - Section blocks utilize expansive vertical separation (`4rem` to `6rem`) to cultivate luxury pacing.
  - Image-driven modules alternate with high-contrast text tiles and pill-contoured gallery layouts.
  - Nested elements within cards strictly follow the internal scale: micro elements align with `space-xs` and `space-sm`, content stacks adhere to `space-md`, and primary card interior padding leverages `space-lg`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on warm tonal layering, delicate ghost outlines, and ultra-diffused luminous shadows rather than traditional heavy drop shadows.

- **Tonal Layering:** The primary canvas (`#FAF8F5`) nests warm white cards (`#FFFFFF`) or subdued cream blocks (`#F5F2EB`). Specialized diagnostic or high-impact modules use rich obsidian charcoal surfaces (`#1A1A1A`) to anchor key moments.
- **Ambient Glow Shadows:** Elevating cards and floating navigations feature feather-light, warm-tinted shadows:
  - Default Elevation: `0px 8px 32px -4px rgba(45, 35, 25, 0.04), 0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(45, 35, 25, 0.02)`.
  - Floating Overlays & Modals: `0px 20px 48px -8px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.08), 0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.03)`.
- **Ghost Outlines:** Containers, pills, and image frames maintain a refined `1px` border set to `#E8DFD5` or `rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.25)`.
- **Editorial Frosted Glass:** Sticky headers and quick-action sheets incorporate frosted glass (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with a `rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.82)` tint and fine champagne border.

## Shapes

The shape system blends sculptural pill elements with softened architectural curves, echoing skin-smoothing treatments and modern clinic interiors:

- **Pill Primitives (`9999px`):** Used universally for action buttons, category tags, notification chips, search capsules, and status indicators.
- **Card & Container Curvature:**
  - Standard cards and treatment modules use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` to `2rem`).
  - Hero image containers and prominent editorial viewports feature sweeping outer corners (`2rem` to `2.5rem`), softening the transition between image and page.
- **Form Controls:** Input fields and selectors mirror button forms with pill geometry or gentle softened borders (`1rem`).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action:** Solid charcoal `#1A1A1A` pill, pure white text, uppercase or sentence case in `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`label-md`). Hover state transitions smoothly to `#2D2D2D` with subtle scale (`scale(1.01)`).
- **Secondary / Champagne Action:** Outline pill with a `1px` border of `#C5A880`, text in `#1A1A1A`. On hover, fills with `#F5F2EB`.
- **Ghost / Minimalist Action:** Transparent background with `#1A1A1A` text and an animated underline that reveals on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact pill height (28px–32px) styled with `label-uppercase`.
- Backed with translucent cream (`rgba(232, 223, 213, 0.45)`) or solid `#F5F2EB`, enclosed in a hairline `#D4C3B3` border.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- **Treatment Cards:** High-aspect-ratio photography with smooth `1.5rem` radius, overlaid with a delicate gradient fade (`rgba(26, 26, 26, 0) 50%` to `rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.65) 100%`) for lower-third Playfair Display titles.
- **Metric & Feature Tiles:** Clean `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F2EB` backgrounds, framed with `1px` `#E8DFD5`, incorporating generous `space-lg` internal padding and prominent numerical callouts.
- **Inverted Dark Tiles:** Select modules (e.g., consultation booking or overview counters) rendered in deep `#1A1A1A` with ivory text to create editorial contrast.

### Form Inputs & Date Pickers
- Pill or rounded (`1rem`) geometry with `#FFFFFF` background, framed in `#E8DFD5`.
- Focus states gently shift the border to champagne gold (`#C5A880`) accompanied by a subtle warm glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.15)`). Placeholder copy is set in `#706E6B`.

### Diagnostic & Inspection Overlays
- Floating white hairline crosshair brackets and pinpoint labels (`1px` `#FFFFFF` with drop shadow) positioned over high-resolution skin photography to highlight bespoke focus areas.
